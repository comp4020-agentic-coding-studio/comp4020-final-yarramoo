    `tools/logo-wordmark.py` (glyphs as ASCII art). Each letter is a sim material (sand, water, lava, moss, ice, fire,
    sand) with a lit top edge, a dark outline, a drop shadow and a few grains falling from the bottoms. Scaled by
    whole numbers with `image-rendering: pixelated`. The `image-gen` alternative could not be tried: the proxy
    returns `HTTP 403 {"detail":"image generation is not enabled for your course"}`.
  - Palette and primitives: `client/menu.css` (`.mn` tokens, `.mn-panel`, `.mn-btn` with sand/lava/water/moss/ghost
    slabs, focus ring, reduced-motion rule). Accents are the sim's own material colours (`client/materials.ts`).

## Design note
Pixel art means: no radius, 2 px "art pixels" for borders and shadows, hard bevels, one pixel font at multiples of
11 px, the logo at integer scale. The backdrop is the game itself, so panels are near-opaque night-purple slabs
(`--mn-panel` at 92%) and never rely on blur. Colour comes from the materials: sand is the primary action, lava is
danger/PvP (Deathmatch), moss is co-op (Survival), water is joining. Screens: title (logo, New game, Join game,
players live, death log), types, settings (themed per type), join (list + code), share (private room link).

## Mobs: findings so far (decision pending)
