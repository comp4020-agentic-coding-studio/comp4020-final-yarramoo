## Design note
Pixel art means: no radius, 2 px "art pixels" for borders and shadows, hard bevels, one pixel font at multiples of
11 px, the logo at integer scale. The backdrop is the game itself, so panels are near-opaque night-purple slabs
(`--mn-panel` at 92%) and never rely on blur. Colour comes from the materials: sand is the primary action, lava is
danger/PvP (Deathmatch), moss is co-op (Survival), water is joining. Screens: title (logo, New game, Join game,
players live, death log), types, settings (themed per type), join (list + code), share (private room link).

## Mobs: findings so far (decision pending)

  (`stepping_set`, `sim/src/enemy.rs`); chunks only update within 576x320 of one (`refresh_active`). A spectator
  camera wakes nothing.

- So option (a) needs a local "explorer" player: Join + Pos, both existing inputs, Pos from the local controller
  (allowed by invariant 9). damage 0 keeps it alive; brushes 1 lets the director paint.
