# Capacity on today's build at the Fly quota (7 Nov)

Labels: [measured] run here, [code] read in code, [inferred] derived. Build `9cfd24f` (multa, **snapshot format 16**,
before world-life's format 17 greenery merge), image `cap-app:9cfd24f`. Format 17 adds static cells at generation only
(no new per-tick work, per the coordinator); generation time, and so the cold-verify cost below, may shift with it.
Raw numbers sit beside this file in `capacity-2026-11-07-*.json`. 

## Headline

**On the sustained quota (6.25% of a core) the server holds no game cleanly. On burst credit (up to a full core) it
holds every mix we tried, up to the 32-member soft cap, but only for as long as the credit lasts: about 30-65 minutes
from a full 500 s balance, and under a minute from the 5 s a fresh machine starts with.**

- **Sustained, credit exhausted:** idle alone uses about half the quota (0.029 core) [measured]. One Survival M room
  of **2** pins the quota (0.061 core, 1601 of 1663 periods throttled). Every snapshot verify takes 10-17 s against the
  10 s timeout, so all 5 were rejected and **none was ever accepted**. Ticks still average 60/s, with a 57.6 minimum
  and event-loop lag p99 of 1.3 s [measured]. Survival of 4 and Deathmatch of 8 fail harder: lag p99 of 2.7-6 s,
  verifies up to 60 s [measured]. **Answer: 0 games.** A small room stays playable with hitches. With no snapshot
  accepted, its tick log is never pruned [code]: every late joiner replays from the last accepted snapshot (or from
  tick 0), memory grows with the log, and `world.log_long` warns at 100 000 ticks (28 min). A full log no longer
  refuses joins (D-100) [code; inferred that it is reached].
- **Burst, credit available:** a core held 60 tps with lag p99 at or under 62 ms through 6 Survival rooms of 4
  (24 members, 0.275 core) and 4 Deathmatch rooms of 8 (32 members, 0.352 core) [measured]. Demand is about **0.034 core
  idle plus about 0.01 core per member**: a Survival room of 4 costs about 0.04 core and a Deathmatch room of 8 about
  0.08 [measured, fitted]. The binding limits are the member cap (32 soft) and memory (cgroup peak 203-205 MB at
  24-32 members, against the server's own 200 MB room-load gate) [measured, see the caveat]. The real limit, though, is
  **how long the credit lasts**.
- **The showcase mix**, 2 Survival rooms of 4 plus 1 Deathmatch room of 8 (16 members), needs about 0.19 core
  [inferred from the fit]. That is **about 65 min** from a full 500 s balance, or **about 40 s** from the 5 s initial
  balance, then the sustained failure above. An idle server banks credit at only about 0.033 s per second, so filling
  500 s takes **about 4-5 h of running idle** (if accrual is quota minus use; the docs give no rate), and `auto_stop_machines` stops an idle machine. The Fly docs do not say
  whether the balance survives a stop [inferred, docs silent].
- **Spikes:** the cold snapshot verify is the spike that matters. With more than one room the verify worker
  **regenerates the world on almost every verify**: it caches one generated base and every room has its own seed
  [code]. A cold verify takes 120-410 ms at M and 0.7-0.9 s at L on a full core, and **6-18 s at M and 35-50 s at L on
  the quota** (always over the 10 s timeout at L). A warm verify is 20-200x cheaper: **0.1-1.6 s at M and 1.1-2.9 s at
  L on the quota** [measured]. A room load costs the server nothing, because it only writes a row; generation runs on
  the clients, at 120-910 ms per client on a full core [code, measured]. A late joiner's catch-up is one cached snapshot
  frame plus at most about 30 s of log, about 80-150 KB sent synchronously [inferred, not measured].

## The Fly shape, re-checked 7 Nov

- One `shared-cpu-1x`, 256 MB, region syd, 1 GB volume [code, `fly.toml`].
- shared vCPU: an 80 ms period with a **5 ms quota (6.25%)**; burst balance **5 s initial, 500 s max**, spent only above
  baseline, bursting at up to 100%; "When the balance is depleted, the vCPU is limited to running at its baseline
  quota", throttled for the rest of the 80 ms period. The page gives no accrual rate and warns the numbers may change
  [docs.fly.io/machines/cpu-performance, fetched 7 Nov].
- Emulation here: Docker `--cpus=0.0625` is CFS quota 6.25 ms per 100 ms, the same share in a slightly longer period
  [inferred: equivalent share; a throttled stall can be up to ~94 ms here and ~75 ms on Fly].

## What the server spends CPU on (from code)

| Path | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `World.tick()` for every loaded world | every 4 ms timer (`TIMER_MS`), all worlds | the two fixed worlds (0 sandbox S, 1 caves L) are always loaded [code, `main.ts:312`] |
| tick close: `encodeTick` + send to every member | 60/s per room | outbound = members x (7 B + inputs x (7 + payload)) x 60; Pos is 16 B, Cast 10 B [code, measured sizes] |
| HASH votes | each member every 60 ticks | majority/truth map |
| tick-log flush to SQLite | 1/s per room (`FLUSH_EVERY_MS`) | one row per flush |
| player stats | one transaction/s | |
| **snapshot verify** | every 1800 ticks (30 s) per room with a live member | worker thread: decompress, `restore`, hash. **`restore` regenerates the world unless the worker's single cached base matches (seed, cfg)** [code, `sim/src/lib.rs` restore, D-088]. Every room has its own random seed (`seedForRoom`), so with two or more rooms verifying, nearly every verify is a full generation [inferred from code] |
| room load (first HELLO) | once per room | `World.load` on a fresh room only writes the world row; the server does **not** generate [code, `world.ts` `loadFromDb`] |
| catch-up for a joiner | once per join | sends the cached snapshot frame plus every logged tick since; send cost only, no sim work [code, `sendCatchUp`] |
| `/api/live` | polled by menus, cached 2 s | |

So the spikes are: verify (generation in the worker), and the catch-up send. Room load is not a server CPU spike; its
generation happens on the clients.

### Spikes [measured unless marked]

**Snapshot verify (the server's only real sim work).** The verify worker's exact job (decompress, restore,
`stateHash`), one reused instance as in `server/verify-worker.ts`. "Cold" is a seed other than the worker's last
restore, so `restore` regenerates the world (D-088 keeps one base per instance). "Warm" is the same seed. "gen" is a
generated snapshot after 60 ticks; "full" has every chunk present, the largest restore input (M about 21 KB, L about
64 KB compressed).

| Preset, size | 1 CPU cold | 1 CPU warm | 0.0625 cold | 0.0625 warm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Survival S | 124-160 ms | 1-4 ms | - | - |
| Survival M | 196-407 ms | 2-7 ms | 12.5-18.5 s | 0.1-1.6 s |
| Survival L | 794-898 ms | 4-46 ms | 39.9-49.6 s | 1.1-2.9 s |
| Deathmatch S | 118-171 ms | 0.4-2 ms | - | - |
| Deathmatch M | 162-596 ms | 1-6 ms | 6.2-11.7 s | 0.1-0.5 s |
| Deathmatch L | 716-792 ms | 4-32 ms | 35.1-42.6 s | 1.1-1.3 s |

(Single-sample cells at 0.0625: 1 cold and 1 warm per row and kind.)

- **Why cold is the common case** [code]: `seedForRoom()` gives every room a random seed, and the verify worker keeps
  one base. With two or more rooms verifying in turn, each verify evicts the other room's base, so every verify
  regenerates. Even one room goes cold after any other room's verify.
- On a full core a cold L verify (0.7-0.9 s) is fine. On the quota **every** L verify and most M verifies exceed the
  10 s `VERIFY_TIMEOUT_MS`, and a rejected verify keeps the log growing [measured].
- The 5 Nov ship check's 9.6 s verify under load is consistent with this [inferred].

**Room load.** On the server, a fresh room's `World.load` writes one row; it does not generate [code]. The cost lands
on each client: world init (generation) was 120-350 ms at S, 190-345 ms at M and 720-910 ms at L on one core in the
image [measured, `initMs`]. A cold room load is therefore not a server spike. Its first verify is (the first-step bump
above).

**Late join / catch-up.** `sendCatchUp` sends the cached snapshot frame (built once per snapshot) and every logged
tick since it, in one synchronous run [code]. With verifies accepted, the log is at most about 30 s plus the request
lead: about 1 900 frames x (7 + inputs per tick x 18 B): a room of 4 at 30 Pos/s each is about 80 KB, a room of 8 about 150 KB, plus the snapshot
(M about 1-21 KB, L about 1-64 KB) [inferred from code and measured sizes]. The send loop costs a few ms of main
thread [inferred, not measured]. **On the quota with no accepted verify, the log grows without bound**: after 30 min a
room of 4 holds about 108 000 frames (about 4.6 MB), all sent and replayed by each joiner [inferred].

## Recommendations (none committed; each is a server change for the session model to weigh)

1. **Verify cold-start is the cheapest big win: make verifies warm.** Options, from cheapest:
   (a) keep one verify sim **per loaded room** (a `Map<roomId, Sim>` in the worker), at about 2 x world cells of
   memory per room (M about 1.6 MB, L about 6.3 MB plus base, D-088). Warm verifies are 20-200x cheaper
   [measured], which brings a quota M verify to 0.1-1.6 s and L to 1-3 s, inside the timeout.
   (b) Order the verify queue by seed so a run of same-seed jobs shares the base. Less memory, less gain.
   This touches the shared input path's trust check, so it goes to review (CLAUDE.md).
2. **Verify less often under load.** `SNAPSHOT_EVERY_TICKS` of 1800 (30 s) is generous when the log is small. 5 min
   (18 000 ticks) cuts verify CPU 10x, and the log stays under about 18 000 frames (about 0.7 MB for a room of 8).
   Or adapt it: skip a cycle when the last verify took over 2 s.
3. **Raise `VERIFY_TIMEOUT_MS` or make it relative.** At the quota a cold M verify is 6-18 s. A timeout of 10 s
   rejects work that would have succeeded, then retries it, which burns the CPU again (5 rejections in 166 s in the
   floor run). 60 s, or "no timeout while the worker is still making progress", wastes less [inferred].
4. **Admit on the burst balance, not only on members.** Read `cpu.stat` (`nr_throttled` rising) and refuse new rooms
   when the machine is throttling. Members are the right unit at a full core but say nothing about credit left.
5. **Caps for the showcase** [inferred]: a total of 16 members (soft) gives about an hour of credit from full. Keep
   `MAX_LOADED_L_ROOMS` at 4, or drop it to 1 until verifies are warm: every cold L verify is 35-50 s at the quota.
6. **Fly's shape, if the course allows it:** a `shared-cpu-1x` cannot hold even one game at its sustained quota.
   `performance-1x` or `shared-cpu-2x` (10 ms/80 ms) is not allowed by the course brief (fixed shape), so this is only
   worth stating in `PROCESS.md` as a known limit [code: fly.toml says leave it].
7. **Ignore memory for now.** The 200 MB gate reads tmpfs-inflated numbers only in this bench. On Fly, recheck with
   `memory.current` from `/api/summary` during the showcase [inferred].

## Architectural decisions for review

- **The headline treats "holds" as 60 tps plus verifies accepted.** A room at the quota with every verify rejected
  still plays (tps mean 60), so "0 games" is about the server's integrity and log growth, not about players seeing a
  frozen game. That reading is a choice; the alternative is "1-2 small rooms play with hitches".
- **Burst duration is computed, not measured.** Docker has no burst balance. The Fly Grafana panel "CPU Quota Balance
  and Throttling" would measure it during a real showcase rehearsal.
- **The bench counts cgroup CPU from outside the container**, so bot CPU never pollutes it, but the bots share the
  host. At steps 3-4 of the 1-CPU runs the host load was 6-8.
