# Spell ideas research

Scope: Noita spells (wiki) and general fun spell designs from other games, ranked against our shape limits (instant at caster, projectile with gravity and impact, area material change, knockback or damage). Noita entries N1 to N14 were read on their wiki pages. Section 2 URLs are search-result pages that were NOT fetched, so their details are snippet-level, and the design details (e.g. Steam Burst, Frost Bloom, Gravity Well, Dust Cloud) are my own adaptations of the game's mechanic, not descriptions from the source. Two wiki pages returned errors and are noted below.

Mana figures are from the Noita wiki as fetched. Tier names follow the brief: starter (unlimited), shallow pickup, deep pickup.

Shape check: Vacuum Field, Gravity Well, and Super Sheep move objects or the player. They need no per-tick search beyond local cells. Homing is avoided throughout.

## 4. Shortlist (8)

| Rank | Spell | Tier | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Circle Of Acid | Starter | Strongest material spell with a simple rule: spread until blocked |
| 2 | Acid Trail | Shallow | Turns any projectile into a digger and a hazard |
| 3 | Chainsaw | Starter | Cheap, efficient digging |
| 4 | Vacuum Field | Shallow | Pull-based control that combos with hazards |
| 5 | Super Sheep | Shallow | Steerable projectile, strong in PvP |
| 6 | Sea Of Lava | Shallow | Area flood that changes the map |
| 7 | Black Hole | Deep | Removes terrain and enables long tunnels |
| 8 | Earthquake | Deep | Large collapse, best reward for digging deep |

Progression: Starter spells (Circle Of Acid, Chainsaw) cover digging and area hazards. Shallow pickups (Acid Trail, Vacuum Field, Super Sheep, Sea Of Lava) add modifiers and control. Deep pickups (Black Hole, Earthquake, plus Cloner or Holy Hand Grenade from the list above) are rare, powerful terrain-removing or chaos spells. Each deeper tier should either remove more terrain or affect a bigger area. Deep spells can carry a bigger drawback, such as a mana cost or a risk to the caster, so depth earns power without making the simple spells obsolete.
