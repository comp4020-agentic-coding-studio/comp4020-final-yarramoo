# Why this place exists

## Not against AI

This site isn't a protest against artificial intelligence. We think AI tools
are remarkable, and we expect they'll keep getting better. We want to help.

Language models learned what they know from text that people wrote: forum
answers, build logs, arguments about the right way to wire a relay. That text
holds more than facts. It holds reasoning, including the false starts, the "I
tried this and it caught fire", and the "here's why the obvious approach
doesn't work". If people stop writing that down, because a machine will answer
for them, the record of how humans actually figure things out stops growing.

So we ask one thing of everyone who posts here: write it yourself. A question
in your own words, a progress update about what really happened on your bench,
an answer that explains how you know. Text like that is worth more than any
amount of fluent filler, to the person reading it today and to whatever learns
from it tomorrow.

## Why the hard way

There's a second reason, and it's about us rather than the machines.

When an answer is always one prompt away, it's easy to stop working things out.
Skills we don't use fade. That's true of soldering, and it's true of thinking.
Knowledge and cognitive atrophy is a real cost of the easy path, and nobody is
going to stop it for us.

The best remedy we know is old-fashioned: take on a hard problem and work
through it, ideally with other people. Hold the torch while someone else
welds. Argue over whether the bracket will flex. Get it wrong together, then
get it right. Figuring things out is far more fun in company than alone, and
it sticks.

## How the site works because of this

- **An honesty pledge, not a police force.** Every post carries a simple
  promise: *I wrote this in my own words.* We trust you to keep it. Nobody here
  is asked to watch anyone else.
- **People first.** Projects need collaborators, questions need answers, and
  teams form in person, around real benches in real suburbs.
- **Labelled for the future.** We publish honest signals about how posts were
  written (see `/provenance.json`), so anyone building datasets can tell
  human-written text from the rest. That's our contribution back to the tools
  we hope to help.

Come and make something hard. Bring a friend.
