## Scene
Sky gradient background, hemisphere + sun, fog, hills. Pad with apron, mount, flame trench, tower (north, empty side), swing arm, blinking beacons, tanks, fence, lamp masts placed between stations. Rocket varies by sequence: mvp is small, kestrel2 is two-stage, kestrel3 is tall with a cargo fairing and boosters. Ring of stations (r=25), one per role, each with a dark silhouette screen, role stripe and flag post; a seated figure only at held seats. Camera sits at your seat (tab switch tweens along the ring); observers get an elevated spectator view. The view is offset so the rocket centres in the visible area beside the panel or sheet.

## State flow
console.js dispatches `mc:state {lobby, myRoles, activeRole, sequenceId}`, `mc:seat`, `mc:layout`, `mc:view`. The scene sees only the public lobby message. `grep views public/scene` matches one comment only. The scene reacts to phase, hold blink, and outcome kind (hides the rocket on success/explosion/terminated/range_violation, red beacons on pad_abort, flame in ascent).

## Layout and fallback
Desktop: 420 px right panel. Phone: bottom sheet, max 55%, collapses to a bar (clock, phase, "Panel"); tapping the bar toggles. 2D when there is no WebGL, reduced motion, or the "2D view" toggle (localStorage, in try/catch). Verified: toggling disposes the canvas.

## Performance (headless, software GL)
5 to 9 draw calls (kestrel3 with five seated figures: 9). 6.4k to 8.4k triangles. 60 fps via `__scene.bench()`. DPR capped at 2 (1.5 under 700 px). Render on demand, paused while hidden.

## Verified
Mission start, all seats, count, hold. Drag turns the view (`__scene.camera`). Observer sees seat changes from another session (`__scene.figures`). Reduced-motion forced 2D. Synthetic ascent and done states render. No browser errors. Screenshots: `3d-P1-desktop.png`, `-desktop-dragged.png`, `-phone.png`, `-phone-collapsed.png`, `-2d.png`, `-kestrel3.png`.

## Weak
Own desk dominates the bottom of the view. Figures are tiny at distance. No-WebGL path untested. The server serves static files without compression and with a 300 s cache, which is awkward for iteration. A seat taken after load does not update `myRoles` without a reload (existing behaviour).

## Ideas
Gzip static assets. Callsign labels (P2). Treat `data-view` quotes carefully: `[data-view=3d]` is invalid CSS and must be quoted.
