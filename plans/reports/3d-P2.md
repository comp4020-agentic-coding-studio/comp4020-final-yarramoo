# 3D P2: presence, gestures, heads

Nothing committed. `pnpm check` green twice on fresh apps (202 tests, typecheck clean).

## Messages (lobby topic, so every stream gets them)
- `lobby` gains `present: { [role]: boolean }` (seat holder has an open stream). Republished 300 ms after a connect or last disconnect.
- `{ type:"gesture", role, kind, at }`, kind in press, call_poll, report_go, report_nogo, hold, resume, scrub, start, terminate. Accepted actions only; the server's abandonment scrub publishes nothing.
- `{ type:"look", heads:{ [role]:[yaw,pitch] } }`, changed roles only, at most 5 Hz per mission. A new stream gets all current heads right after its lobby and views messages.
- `POST /m/:code/look` (`_csrf`, `role`, `yaw`, `pitch`): 403 unheld, 400 non-finite, 409 done, 429 over 8/s per crew.

## Yaw convention (figures.js header)
Wire yaw is relative to facing the pad, positive turning left (three.js `rotation.y` sense), wrapped to (-pi, pi]; world camera yaw = seat angle + yaw. Pitch positive looks up, clamped to +-1.2. Each viewer applies it in the figure's own frame; the neck stops at +-1.3 rad.

## Coarse by construction
`gestureKind(action)` reads only `action.type` (and the already-public go/no-go). A fix step and a normal step are both "press". `spec/presence.test.ts` checks every gesture's keys are exactly `type, role, kind, at`, and no key or value equals any step, value, fault or poll id or label. It also covers rejection (no gesture), presence on/off within 1.5 s, look validation, the 429, and a 5 Hz coalescing bound.

## Bounds
Presence map exists only while streams are open. Heads: one entry per role per mission, cleared when the mission ends, swept after 10 min with no streams. Nothing persisted.

## Client
console.js forwards `mc:gesture` and `mc:look`, and posts `mc:mylook` at 4 Hz or less, only after more than 0.02 rad of change, only in 3D. Measured: 9 posts in 2 s of continuous dragging. New `public/scene/figures.js`: jointed figures, damped poses, eight gestures, canvas-sprite labels, away state (translucent, slumped, "away"). Figures are now 2x scale, with a matching chair. 21 draw calls, 6.7k triangles with three figures.

## Weak spots

- Presence is per callsign, so two tabs of one crew count once.
