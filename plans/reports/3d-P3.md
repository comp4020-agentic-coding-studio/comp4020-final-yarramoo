# 3D P3: the launch moment

Nothing committed. `pnpm check` green on a fresh app at :8112 (202 tests).

## Built
New `public/scene/`: `effects.js` (all of it), `clock.js` (pad board), `particles.js` (pooled InstancedMesh sets). Edited `pad.js` (tower, swing arm and beacons are now their own group), `figures.js` and `stations.js` (watch, flinch, cheer reactions), `scene.js` (hooks, camera follow and shake, `__scene.preview`), `console.js` (publishes public `clockMs/clockAt/clockPhase`).
- Pad clock canvas board (T-/T+, COUNT, TERMINAL COUNT, amber HOLD, outcome word), redrawn at most 4x/s.
- Count: vent puffs from T-10 s, ignition flicker from T-1.5 s, arm swings away at T-30 s.
- Liftoff: additive two-cone flame with flicker, pooled smoke on the pad, trail, ease-in climb, shake scaled by distance, camera tilts up after 3 s without input. Explosion: flash, fireballs, ballistic debris, scorch, leaning tower. Pad abort: flicker, shutdown, white smoke. Scrub: dimmer light, arm back in. Terminated: sky flash, falling debris. Range violation: distant flash. Success: dot in the sky, figures cheer.

## State to visuals
Everything is a function of phase, extrapolated tMs, outcome kind, outcome.atT and now. A page that opens after an outcome skips straight to the tableau (scorch, tilt, landed debris, no rocket). Particles only spawn forward from "now".

## The arc never depends on anomalies
`pathAt(p)` takes only a fraction. p = T+ over `ascentSec` (60 for Kestrel-3, a static table), or 12 s of cinematic. Nothing but phase and clock reaches it. `__scene.arc(0.35)` returns identical values before and after any outcome preview. The outcome only decides where the rocket stops on that arc.

## Performance
28 draw calls and 18k triangles during a Kestrel-1 explosion; 43 and 19.8k for Kestrel-3 with five figures. Particle cap 350 in three pools, no allocation per frame. The loop sleeps when idle. Reduced motion skips shake and flashes.

## Verified
Via preview: clock, liftoff, explosion (live, 1 s and 7 s in, and 60 s late), pad abort, Kestrel-3 ascent at T+20, terminated, range violation, success cheer. Live: a real Kestrel-1 mission scrubbed (`3d-P3-scrub-live.png`). No JS errors. I did not run a real 4-minute count to T-0.

## Weak spots
- The ascent length is hardcoded (the lobby message lacks `ascentSec`).
- Puffs are chunky up close.
- Late joiners at an old outcome see no effects by design.
- The board can hide the tower from some seats.
- Scrub dimming is subtle.
