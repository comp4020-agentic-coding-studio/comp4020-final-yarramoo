# 3D P4: the panel as an object, and scene fixes

Nothing committed. `pnpm typecheck` clean; `pnpm check` green (202 tests) against a fresh app on :8113.

## Changed
- `style.css`: each console is a painted-metal chassis (gradients, bevel, four screws), a role-colour stencil strip ("PROPULSION · STATION 2"), engraved labels, switch-style steps, analogue meters (bezel, ticks, seven-segment-style readout, amber lamp for caution), indicator lamps, guarded GO / NO-GO pair and hazard-bordered HOLD / RESUME / SCRUB / TERMINATE, a paper clipboard logbook for the Flight Director, nameplate seats, a typed incident report with a stamped outcome. The 2D view uses the same DOM and looks the same.
- `console.js`: markup only (stencil, lamps, classes), plus +/- zoom buttons; "Look" also recentres.
- Scene: own flag post hidden; ascent length read from `data-ascent-sec` (table removed); zoom 1x to 2.8x (68 to 24 degrees) by wheel, pinch, buttons; double-click or double-tap resets.
- `server.ts`: `no-cache` outside production.

## Accessibility
Buttons stay real `<button>`s with the same data attributes, at least 56 px (44 px for tabs and zoom). Lit buttons are dark text on bright role colour (above 7:1); locked rows are light grey on near-black (about 6:1). Every lit state carries text too (DONE, MISSED, NORMAL, GO), never colour alone. The glow pulse runs only under `no-preference`.

## Board
It hangs on a boom from the tower, high up and 10 m off the rocket's axis, on the same side of the tower as your seat so the tower never hides it. Checked from all five Kestrel-3 seats: it never covers the rocket or the tower, and is readable (flank seats see it at the top of the frame).

## Verified
Desktop, flight logbook, phone sheet, collapsed bar, zoom on a neighbour, 2D, and five seat views (`3d-P4-*.png`). Look reset, wheel, double-click reset, +/- checked. No JS errors.

## Weak spots
- Touch pinch and double-tap are untested on a real device.
- The phone sheet hides the phase badge while open.
- The board sits near the top of the frame from the flank seats.
- Lit-contrast figures are estimates, not measured.
